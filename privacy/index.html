Feather Widgets Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Feather Widgets is a desktop widget app for Windows, made by Ammar Arquil, an individual developer. This policy explains what the app keeps on your PC, what it sends over the internet and why, and how to delete it.

The short version: Feather Widgets works without an account and contains no ads, analytics or tracking. Your widgets, notes, lists and settings stay on your PC. A few widgets fetch information from the internet, and each one sends only what it needs to get that information.

What stays on your PC

Everything the app saves is stored in one folder inside your Windows user profile:

  • Store version: %LocalAppData%\Packages\AmmarArquil.FeatherWidgets_azstgk5z76jjp\LocalCache\Local\WidgetsApp\
  • Direct-download version: %LocalAppData%\WidgetsApp\

That folder holds:

  • state.json: which widgets are on your desktop, their position, size and settings. This includes anything you type into a widget, such as notes, to-do items, habits, the calculator's last sum, countdown names and labels. It also holds:
    • the place you chose for Weather, Prayer Times or Sun & Moon (town name, latitude, longitude and time zone);
    • the last exchange rates the Currency widget downloaded, and the amount in its converter;
    • Screen Time's activity log (minutes active per hour, last 14 days);
    • the paths of folders, files and apps you pick for Pinned Folder, Photo Slideshow and App Launcher;
    • your calendar link for Agenda, encrypted with Windows' own data protection (DPAPI) so it can only be read by your Windows account on this PC.
  • gametime.json: Game Time's record of which games you played (game name and program path) and for how long, last 14 days. It is only recorded while a Game Time widget is on your desktop.
  • net-today.txt: Network Speed's total of data downloaded and uploaded today.
  • media\: a copy of the picture or GIF you chose for Your GIF, and icons App Launcher extracted from the apps you added.
  • error.log: technical details when something goes wrong inside the app, so a broken widget doesn't crash the rest. It can contain file paths from your PC. It is capped at about 256 KB and is never sent anywhere.

Some widgets read information from your PC to show it to you, and keep it in memory only: CPU, memory and network use (PC Monitor, Top Apps, Network Speed), battery level, disk space, your PC's specifications and name (System Info hides the name unless you turn it on), the files in a folder you pinned, your desktop wallpaper (used as a backdrop in the widget gallery), and the names of running programs (Top Apps lists them; if you turn on Lighter glass "With Wallpaper Engine or Lively", the app also checks whether one of those is running, so it can switch off the blur). None of it leaves your PC.

The app also writes to the Windows registry under your user account so it can start with Windows (you can turn this off from the tray icon or in Windows Settings > Apps > Startup).

What is sent over the internet, and why

Feather Widgets only connects to the internet for the widgets below, and only while one of them is on your desktop or open in the gallery. Every request carries a plain app identifier (Feather Widgets/ and the version number) and, like any internet connection, your IP address. Nothing else about you or your PC is sent.

Clicking "Based on data from MET Norway" in the Weather widget's menu, or a website shortcut you added to App Launcher, opens that site in your web browser. What happens there is covered by that site's own policy.

The services' own privacy terms

  • MET Norway: api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService and met.no/en/about-us/privacy. MET Norway says its access logs store your IP address with the coordinates in the request, kept in its own data centre in Oslo, and that IP addresses are stored for up to 90 days.
  • Frankfurter: frankfurter.dev. Frankfurter says it doesn't collect or log personal data, IP addresses or request URLs. It runs behind Cloudflare, which only provides aggregate traffic statistics.
  • Your calendar provider: your calendar link goes to the provider you already use, under that provider's privacy policy (for example Google's or Microsoft's).
  • Ping servers: the server you choose receives the echo under its operator's own policy.

What Feather Widgets never collects

The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking code, and it does not send crash reports. Nothing you type, no file, photo, note, list, calendar event, activity log or game history is ever uploaded. Ammar Arquil receives no data from the app.

Microsoft runs the Microsoft Store. When you install or use an app from the Store, Microsoft may share summary information with developers, such as download counts and Windows crash reports, under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Feather Widgets adds nothing to that.

Notifications

Some widgets show a Windows notification from the tray icon (for example when a timer ends, a prayer time begins or a Pro preview ends). These are created on your PC and use no notification service on the internet.

Deleting your data

  • Remove one widget: right-click it and choose Remove from desktop. Its settings, including anything you typed into it, are deleted from state.json. Game Time's history, Network Speed's daily total, and pictures or icons copied into media\ stay in the data folder until you delete it or uninstall.
  • Disconnect your calendar: in Agenda, choose Connect calendar… > Disconnect. The link is deleted.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling Feather Widgets from Windows Settings > Apps removes the app and its data folder listed above.
  • Delete manually: quit Feather Widgets from its tray icon, then delete the data folder listed above. Next time the app starts, it starts fresh.

Data held by MET Norway, Frankfurter or your calendar provider is covered by their own policies, linked above.

Children

Feather Widgets is a general-audience utility. It does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently (for example when Pro purchases arrive through the Microsoft Store), this policy will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
