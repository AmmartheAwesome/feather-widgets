Which one suits you

Rainmeter

Rainmeter's skins are made and shared by its users, and you can change every part of one. It suits people who like editing files and want full control. It is free and open source, and it still runs on Windows 7. It comes with a few starter skins. Most others you download and install, and changing a skin beyond its own settings means editing its text file.

BeWidgets

BeWidgets is a free Microsoft Store app. You set its widgets up inside the app and can download more from its own widget store. Some features need its paid Premium add-on.

Widget Launcher

Widget Launcher has been on the Microsoft Store since 2013. Its basic widgets are free, and you buy extra widgets and skins one by one, or in a $4.89 Feature Pack. Its listing offers paid add-ons to remove ads.

Feather Widgets for Windows

Feather Widgets is for people who want ready-made widgets, without files or skins. The widgets are frosted glass and sit on the wallpaper behind your apps. The free set has 42 widgets with no time limit and no ads, including Prayer Times and a PC monitor. It is newer and smaller than Rainmeter, and it only runs on Windows 10 and 11.
